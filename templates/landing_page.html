About

Connect-to-Course is a project that tends to solve the problem of going to different online platforms to learn. It helps to bring all your learning onto a single platform. This project was initiated to help learners search for courses online and save the courses they intend to learn. With this, they can focus on learning, when they have saved all the courses they intend to learn to improve on or start their career.

Currently the project showcases top curated courses and continues expanding its scope. Developed as a full-stack web development portfolio project.
