Our Story

About Connect-to-Course

Connect-to-Course was created to solve a common pain point: online learners constantly bouncing between fragmented platforms, tutorials, and bookmarks. By unifying search, discovery, and personal bookmarking onto a single reliable application, learners can curate their curriculum and focus on developing career-ready skills.

Initiated as a full-stack portfolio capstone project for the ALX Software Engineering program, Connect-to-Course showcases end-to-end Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL, and modern responsive front-end engineering.
